# GhostLayer — Experience Direction

## Three potential directions

### Theme Name: The Instrument Panel
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, editorial systems console that treats live infrastructure intelligence as something operators can inspect rather than merely admire. The mood is calm, precise, and high-trust.
**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Compute Observatory
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark scientific field guide where technical phenomena arrive as measurable observations across a vast compute landscape. The mood is expansive, contemplative, and research-led.
**Probability:** 0.09

### Theme Name: Ledger of Motion
**Very Brief Intro:** A print-inspired decision ledger that turns every optimization into a clear, auditable record of cause, action, verification, and savings. The mood is archival, analytical, and disciplined.
**Probability:** 0.02

## Chosen approach: The Instrument Panel

### Design Movement
**Contemporary editorial systems design** with the restraint of technical field manuals and the compositional confidence of a control room. It removes the clichéd “futuristic SaaS” language in favor of information that earns attention through clarity, hierarchy, and tactility.

### Core Principles
1. **Show the mechanism, not a marketing metaphor.** The experience invites visitors to drive a small, legible version of GhostLayer’s decision loop.
2. **Make the technical tangible.** Short explanatory language sits beside live visual evidence, so terms such as baseline, policy, and rollback become obvious through interaction.
3. **Use motion as causality.** Animation traces how an observation becomes a verified intervention; it never exists as ambient decoration.
4. **Maintain productive restraint.** Large type, generous air, deliberate edges, and a limited color vocabulary create authority without the conventional dark-neon startup aesthetic.

### Color Philosophy
The interface uses **paper-like mineral white and soft graphite** as the trusted operating environment. A deep **ghost teal** acts as the owned signal color for verified improvement, while rust red is reserved exclusively for detected waste and unsafe outcomes. This means color functions as a language of system state rather than a decorative palette.

### Layout Paradigm
The page is a **progressive field report**: a left-aligned editorial rail introduces the problem, while a wide instrument stage on the right lets visitors manipulate the decision loop. Subsequent sections alternate between evidence strips, full-bleed diagrams, and side annotations—avoiding a stack of interchangeable centered cards.

### Signature Elements
- **The decision trace:** a horizontal causal line that physically follows a signal from detection through a safety gate to an outcome.
- **The node field:** an interactive lattice of compute nodes that changes state without pretending to be a generic dashboard.
- **Margin notes:** quiet, monospace explanatory labels that translate specialist terms at the exact moment they appear.

### Interaction Philosophy
Visitors learn by changing one variable and seeing the system respond. Small controls—baseline versus guided mode, a node selection, and a risk scenario—return immediate, readable feedback. Hover and scroll feel tactile, but complex claims always have a plain-language companion and a visible qualification.

### Animation
**Motion** handles state transitions, sequencing, scroll entrances, and interface presence with springy but restrained movement. **Anime.js** powers the decision trace, node-field propagation, metric count transitions, and focus pulses when the visitor runs an intervention. Motion stays under 420ms for ordinary interface changes; the causal trace may use a slower 700–900ms sweep to make an action understandable. Motion is disabled or simplified when reduced-motion preferences are active.

### Typography System
**DM Mono** carries metrics, labels, and system evidence in compact uppercase. **Instrument Serif** creates human, high-trust narrative headlines with confident contrast. **Manrope** carries explanatory copy in an open, contemporary rhythm. Headings are left aligned at large scale; technical detail uses narrow mono labels and generous line spacing rather than dense panel walls.

### Brand Essence
**GhostLayer is the decision intelligence layer for ML infrastructure teams that need to improve GPU efficiency without losing control.**

Personality adjectives: **auditable, composed, incisive**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines should be direct and evidence-led; CTAs should invite an inspection or a controlled test rather than promise a generic transformation. Microcopy explains exactly what will change and what will remain safeguarded.

Example lines: “See the decision before it touches production.” and “Run the same workload. Keep the evidence.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a **three-plane ghost stack**: offset, partially overlapping rectangular layers whose negative space forms an implied G. It communicates memory, orchestration, and a system operating just above the visible layer—without resorting to a shield, chip, or generic spark.

### Signature Brand Color
**Ghost Teal — #087E79.** A mineral, blue-green verification signal that remains sophisticated on light surfaces and unmistakable in system states.

## Style Decisions

The home experience is now **globe-led**. A responsive, navigable three-dimensional globe sits inside a predominantly black-and-white space field and serves as the primary way to enter the site’s core destinations. The previous paper-like editorial surface remains available inside focused information panels, but it no longer controls the first impression.

The globe’s surface stays graphite, white, and atmospheric gray. **Ghost Teal** means a verified or healthy system path, while **rust red** denotes a constrained or risky condition. These colors must remain sparse and functional rather than becoming a broad decorative palette.

The navigation model is spatial: selecting a hot-bar destination rotates and eases the globe toward its matching marker, zooms to the signal, then opens a concise associated panel. Selecting a marker performs the same behavior. An explicit close control returns the interface to orbital view so visitors can move directly from one destination to another without resetting the experience.

The orbital field is explicitly an **infrastructure instrument**, not a cosmos motif. Its teal causal trace communicates Detect → Verify → Preserve, while visible operational labels, policy states, and evidence records frame every marker as a bounded decision rather than a scenic point of interest. Each major section carries a field-manual artifact—an audit fragment, margin note, causal trace, or evidence strip—and the three-plane mark is echoed in panel geometry and instrument labels.
