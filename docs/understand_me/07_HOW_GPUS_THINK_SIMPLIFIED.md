# 🧠 07: How GPUs Work (Simplified)

What is a GPU anyway?

### 👨‍🏫 CPU vs. GPU Explained with Minions!

- **CPU (Normal Computer Brain)**: Think of a CPU as **1 super genius professor**. He can solve hard math problems very fast, but he can only solve **one problem at a time**.
- **GPU (Graphics Computer Brain)**: Think of a GPU as **10,000 energetic Minions**! Each Minion does super simple math, but they all work **at the exact same time**!

---

### 🎨 Why GPUs are Perfect for AI
AI is just millions of tiny multiplication problems (like painting millions of tiny pixels on a screen). 

The 10,000 Minions (GPU cores) can paint the whole picture in 1 second! 

**Ghost Layer's Job**: Make sure all 10,000 Minions get their paint buckets instantly so nobody stands around doing nothing! 🎨
