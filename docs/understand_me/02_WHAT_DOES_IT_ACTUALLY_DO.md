# 🤖 02: What Does Ghost Layer Actually Do?

Think of building an AI like baking 1,000,000 cookies. 

If you use a small spoon, put one cookie in the oven at a time, and keep checking the recipe book every 5 seconds, it will take **100 years** to bake all the cookies!

### 🍪 Ghost Layer's 4 Simple Steps

1. **👀 Look (Observe)**: It watches the computer while it bakes cookies. It notices: *"Hey! The oven is empty because the chef is taking too long to mix the dough!"*
2. **🧠 Spot the Problem (Diagnose)**: It finds out exactly why things are slow.
3. **⚡ Fix It (Safe-Apply)**: It uses bigger baking sheets, turns up the heat safely, and passes the dough to the oven twice as fast!
4. **🛡️ Taste Test (Verify)**: It tastes a cookie to make sure it's still delicious and hasn't burned! If anything tastes weird, it instantly turns the oven back to normal!

---

### 🎁 The End Result
The computer bakes all 1,000,000 cookies in **half the time**, tastes exactly right, and costs way less money!
